The two-dimensional aerodynamic trajectory model is defined in Appendix A, Section A.1.1 of FAA Report DOT/FAA/AR-99/11, Large Engine Uncontained Debris Analysis (May 1999), specifically on pages A-1 and A-2. Figure A-1 in that appendix presents the selected coordinate system for the debris trajectory model. The equations for relative wind, drag, drag components, accelerations, and the explicit Euler integration scheme are given on page A-1 and continue onto page A-2. The drag coefficient of 1.17 for a tumbling rectangular plate, with an average tumbling orientation of 45° for frontal area, is also specified on page A-2. The same appendix is reproduced in ATSB Investigation AO-2010-089 (Qantas A380, Batam Island), where it is also labeled Appendix A and describes the model identically.
