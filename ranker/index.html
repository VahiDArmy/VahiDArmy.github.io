Scoring model

Each dimension is scored from 0 to 100. Its weight changes its contribution to the weighted overall score. Keep dimensions distinct: a high score in one trait should not silently compensate for a low score in another when inspecting the profile.
The overall score is a convenience for sorting. The dimension vector is the actual profile.
